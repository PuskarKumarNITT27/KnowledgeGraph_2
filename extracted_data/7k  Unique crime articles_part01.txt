Image used for representative purpose only MUMBAI: An 18-year-old man was arrested by the crime branch on Monday on charges of abduction and molestation of an underage girl. The two teenagers got acquainted with one another online, the police said. The 14-year-old girl had met the accused man on social media about a month ago. The two had become friends on and they would chat often, the police said. The girl, who studies in school and lives with her family at Bandra, had been upset with her family as they planned to move her to a boarding school. On Saturday night, the man picked up the girl near her house on his scooter. He then took her to Kashmira after midnight where he forcibly touched her inappropriately. On Sunday morning, he dropped her back to the western suburbs on his scooter. All this time, her family was worried since she had not returned home the entire night. Her family had approached Bandra police station and registered a missing person's complaint. According to Supreme Court guidelines, every case of a missing minor has to be registered as abduction. Accordingly, the police registered an abduction complaint. When the girl returned home on Sunday, her family members questioned her of her whereabouts. She informed them that her social media 'friend' had taken her to Kashimira and forcibly touched her inappropriately. Officials of Bandra police station then added charges of molestation to the case. As the girl is underage, charges were added under the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Act. Officials of unit 11 of the crime branch of the city police said they had received information that the man lives in Dahisar (east). He was arrested and handed over to officials of Bandra police station on Monday. (The victim's identity has not been revealed to protect her privacy as per Supreme Court directives on cases related to sexual assault)
